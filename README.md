## Finance Game Simulator

You play in a simulated world economy with companies, banks and other people.

The people are autonomous algorithmic agents that will play with the same knowledge and tools as you.

You can invest in stocks, place your money into savings or create companies.

With thousands of agents and companies you will have no problem having an original gameplay every time you play the game AND by resetting you will have a totally different economic situation.

You have one objective: BE THE RICHEST!!!

Ideated, architectured and programmed by me because I was bored before the start of the Piscine of Ecole 42 Paris.

The code is completely open-source and written in C. To play the game just clone the repo in local and in terminal while being in the repo local foler type 'make' (for the next times just time ./Game cause you already compiled it).
